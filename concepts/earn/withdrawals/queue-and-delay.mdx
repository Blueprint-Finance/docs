An Earn V2 Vault settles withdrawals asynchronously. When a user submits a
withdraw request, the request is added to the open epoch's queue and is
paid out at that epoch's payout. The interval between submission and
payout is the withdrawal delay.

What the user is promised:

- The request is recorded on submission with a queue place (oldest = 1).
- The request is paid out at the next payout that falls after the
  epoch's cutoff, subject to any cap rules (see
  [Withdrawal caps](./caps-concept)).
- Until paid, the request can be cancelled by the user; once cancelled
  it releases the user's share balance.

The duration of the delay is communicated to the user in three forms:

- The CMS field `withdrawalQueueDelay`, a human-readable summary
  (for example, "up to 7 days") shown in the queue card prose.
- The CMS field `withdrawalQueueDescription`, an optional rich-text
  override for vaults whose schedule needs extra explanation.
- The dynamically computed next payout date, derived from the vault's
  withdrawal cron and current epoch (see
  [Epoch lifecycle](./epoch-cycle-concept)).

The summary and description are editorial copy. The dynamic date is the
load-bearing value: it determines when assets actually become claimable
for a given request.
