# Withdrawal disable

A vault may disable withdrawals in the UI. The withdraw form is hidden
or rendered in a disabled state, but existing positions remain on-chain
and are unaffected by the UI state.

When withdrawals are not disabled, the vault processes them through its
standard flow:

- On an Earn V1 vault, calling withdraw returns the underlying asset
  synchronously in the same transaction, provided the vault holds
  enough liquidity. There is no queue and no epoch.
- On an Earn V2 vault, calling withdraw queues the request into the
  next applicable epoch, and the request settles at that epoch's
  payout. See [Withdrawal queue and delay](./queue-and-delay).

Reasons a vault may disable withdrawals:

- Pre-deposit vaults: deposits are accepted on a source chain before
  the destination vault is live. Withdrawals stay disabled until the
  LayerZero claim window opens on the destination chain. See
  [Pre-deposit claim](../cross-chain/pre-deposit-claim).
- Migrations: the operator pauses withdrawals while moving strategy or
  contracts.
- Contract-level limit changes that would make withdrawals
  non-functional, for example a liquidity threshold that no longer
  holds.

Deposit disable and withdrawal disable are independent. A vault
commonly has deposits disabled while withdrawals remain enabled. That
is the wind-down state used during soft-close or deprecation.
