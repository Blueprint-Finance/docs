A vault's withdrawal cron schedule can change over time, for example
moving from weekly to bi-weekly payouts. The release-candidate (RC)
config makes those transitions predictable so existing requests are not
silently re-paced.

Every Earn V2 Vault may carry an optional release-candidate withdrawal
config alongside its active config. The RC config has a `startingFrom`
timestamp that marks the boundary at which the new schedule takes over.
Epochs whose `startBlockTimestamp` is before `startingFrom` follow the
previous schedule end-to-end: both their cutoff and their payout compute
from the previous cron. Epochs whose `startBlockTimestamp` is at or
after `startingFrom` follow the new schedule end-to-end. The schedule
never changes mid-epoch. The transition boundary is always the start of
an epoch.

When the RC config is active or imminent, the Withdrawal Queue card may
surface a "Learn more" link, set in the CMS as
`withdrawalRcLearnMoreLink`, pointing to the operator's explainer for
the specific change.

Vaults without an RC config behave normally: the active config governs
every epoch. See [Withdrawal epoch lifecycle](./epoch-cycle-concept) for
how each epoch progresses under a single schedule.
