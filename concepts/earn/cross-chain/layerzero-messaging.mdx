# Cross-chain messaging (LayerZero)

Some Concrete vaults span multiple chains: you interact with the
vault on a source chain, but the underlying strategy or share balance
lives on a destination chain. The bridge between the two is built on
**LayerZero**, a generic cross-chain messaging protocol.

What you are promised:

- A cross-chain action (e.g. claiming pre-deposit shares, cross-chain
  withdrawal) emits a message from the source-chain contract. LayerZero
  carries the message to the destination chain, where the receiving
  contract executes the corresponding action.
- Cross-chain actions are **payable**: you pay an additional fee
  on the source chain to cover destination-chain execution and
  LayerZero verifier costs. The UI quotes this fee before you
  sign and applies a small safety buffer so transient gas-price
  movement does not cause the transaction to revert.
- Delivery is asynchronous. After the source-chain transaction
  confirms, there is a delay (typically minutes, occasionally longer
  under network congestion) before the destination-chain state
  updates. The UI surfaces this delay rather than implying instant
  settlement.
- Funds are not at risk during the in-flight period in the sense of
  custody. The source-chain action has already burned or locked your
  position before the message is sent. You cannot act on the
  destination-chain side until the message is delivered.

LayerZero is used today for **pre-deposit share claims** (see
[Pre-deposit claim](./pre-deposit-claim)) and for **cross-chain
withdrawals** on vaults whose underlying strategy executes on a
different chain than you deposit from. Additional flows may use
the same primitive in the future; the user-facing pattern (quote → sign
on source → wait for delivery → use on destination) is shared across
them.

For protocol-level detail on options, executor settings, and fee
estimation, see the smart-contract repository's LayerZero documentation.
