# Vault rendering controls

Before any vault renders to users in production, Concrete applies layered
rendering controls so an accidental CMS change or an unreviewed vault
story cannot expose untrusted contracts. The same controls also let
Concrete preview new vaults end-to-end in non-production environments
without affecting the production surface.

## Address allowlist

Every contract address attached to a vault story must appear on the
maintained address allowlist before that vault story renders on the
production Earn surface. Adding a vault story in the CMS is not enough
on its own; the allowlist is reviewed as part of the vault release
process and acts as a safety floor against accidental or malicious CMS
edits.

What this means in practice:

- Until a vault's addresses are on the allowlist, the vault is silently
  excluded from the production Earn surface.
- The allowlist is intended as a safety floor, not as the primary
  access-control mechanism for any specific user.
- Non-production environments and draft mode bypass the allowlist so the
  team can preview new vaults end-to-end before promoting them.

## Visibility flags

A vault story may also be flagged in two ways at the CMS level:

- `hiddenCard` on the vault. The vault does not appear as a card on the
  Earn landing, but the per-vault page remains reachable by direct link.
  Use this to surface a vault that should not be marketed but is still
  operational for known holders.
- `hidden` on the parent group. The group and every vault inside it are
  excluded from the Earn landing in production. Hidden groups remain
  visible in draft mode and isolated environments so they can be
  reviewed before promotion.

These flags are presentation controls layered on top of the address
allowlist. A card-visible vault still drops from production if its
contract addresses are not on the allowlist; conversely, an allowlisted
vault that is flagged `hiddenCard` is allowed by the safety floor but
presented as unlisted.

You do not interact with either layer directly. The only observable
effect is that a vault becomes available on the Earn surface once it has
been reviewed and approved for production.
