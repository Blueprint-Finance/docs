# Vault address whitelist

The Earn app does not blindly trust the CMS. Before any vault is rendered
to users in production, every contract address attached to it must pass a
**maintained allowlist** of vaults known to the team.

What this means in practice:

- Adding a vault story in the CMS is not, on its own, enough to expose the
  vault to users. Until the vault's addresses are on the allowlist, the
  vault is silently excluded from the production Earn surface.
- The allowlist is reviewed and updated as part of the vault release
  process. It is intended as a safety floor against accidental or
  malicious CMS changes, not as the primary access-control mechanism.
- Non-production environments and draft mode bypass the allowlist so the
  team can preview new vaults end-to-end before promoting them.

Users do not interact with the allowlist directly. Its only observable
effect is that a vault becomes available on the Earn surface once it has
been reviewed.
