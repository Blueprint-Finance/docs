A Concrete vault's headline APY is computed from live data so the figure
reflects recent strategy performance. The number you see on the vault card
and on the vault page is derived from the vault's own activity, not a value
hard-coded in the CMS.

## Inputs to the calculation

Two sources feed the dynamic APY:

- On-chain accrual recorded by the vault contract, which captures how
  share value has moved as the strategy earned yield.
- A subgraph-indexed performance history over a recent window, which
  smooths short-lived spikes and provides the series used to derive an
  annualised rate.

The two inputs combine into a current annualised yield. The figure
refreshes between visits and reflects the most recently observed
performance.

## Relationship to `aprTarget`

A pinned `aprTarget` value from the CMS may still exist on the vault.
Under dynamic APY, `aprTarget` is supplementary context (for example, the
target the strategy was launched with) and is not used as the headline.
The headline is always derived from the live calculation when dynamic APY
is enabled.

## When dynamic APY is not enabled

For vaults where dynamic APY is not enabled, the headline shows the pinned
`aprTarget` value instead and is stable between CMS updates. The
pinned-value path is documented in the per-vault Institutional APY
section; see the per-vault [dynamic-apy](./dynamic-apy) page for vaults
where it applies.

## Historical chart

A historical APY chart may render on the vault page independently of the
headline-APY source. The presence of the chart is controlled by a
separate flag, so a vault can show a chart without enabling dynamic APY
and the reverse is also possible.
