A single vault product can represent **multiple contract deployments**. For
example, the same strategy on several chains, or several deployments of the
same strategy on one chain. Each deployment is identified by its contract
address.

What the user sees:

- One vault entry on the Earn landing.
- One vault page, with an address selector when more than one deployment is
  available.
- Per-address state for balance, deposit, and withdraw flows. Switching
  the selector switches the on-chain target.

Deployments share the CMS content (overview, auditors, owner, rewards,
images) but each has its own on-chain state. APY, balance, and TVL are
computed per address.
