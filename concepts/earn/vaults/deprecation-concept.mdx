# Vault deprecation

A deprecated vault is no longer being maintained. Concrete marks the vault
as deprecated to signal that it is being wound down, so you can make an
informed decision about your position.

## What deprecation means in practice

Deprecation is a business signal Concrete sets in the CMS. When a vault is
deprecated:

- A visible deprecation indicator renders next to the vault on the Earn
  landing and on the per-vault page.
- Deposits are typically disabled (see
  [Deposit disable](../deposits/disable-concept)).
- The vault is not promoted to new users and stops appearing in feature
  placements.
- Existing holders can still withdraw their shares as long as the underlying
  contract permits withdrawals. The deposit-disable gate is independent of
  the withdrawal-disable gate (see
  [Withdrawal disable](../withdrawals/disable-concept)).

## Group-level deprecation

When a vault group is marked deprecated, deprecation propagates to every
vault inside that group. Each vault in the group behaves as if deprecated
even if its own flag is not set.

## Relationship to on-chain state

Deprecation is a product signal, not a contract state. Whether the vault
remains operational at the contract level depends on on-chain state,
including whether deposits and withdrawals are explicitly disabled. The
product surface and the on-chain surface are independent layers.
