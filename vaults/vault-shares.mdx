When you deposit into a Concrete vault, you receive `ct[Asset]` tokens in return. These tokens are your vault shares — a yield-bearing receipt that represents your proportional ownership of the vault's underlying assets. Each vault sets its own ERC-20 share name and symbol at deployment (for example, a Senior USDC vault might issue shares with the symbol `ctSrUSDC`). The exact symbol you receive depends on the specific vault you deposit into. The tokens sit in your wallet like any other ERC-20; you do not need to do anything with them for yield to accrue.

## How share price is calculated

Your `ct[Asset]` token balance stays fixed after your deposit. What changes is the share-to-asset exchange rate, calculated as:

```text
exchange rate = total vault assets ÷ total shares outstanding
```

As the vault earns yield, total assets increase. With the same number of shares outstanding, each share is redeemable for more of the underlying asset. When you withdraw, your `ct[Asset]` shares are redeemed against the exchange rate that applies at the time the withdrawal is settled — for vaults with an asynchronous withdrawal queue (the most common configuration), this is the share price locked at the time the epoch is processed.

## ct\[Asset\] tokens are standard ERC-20

`ct[Asset]` tokens follow the ERC-20 standard, meaning they can be freely transferred to any address. Whoever holds the tokens at the time of redemption holds the claim on the underlying assets. Note that transferring `ct[Asset]` shares does **not** transfer any pending withdrawal requests. If you have an unprocessed withdrawal request and transfer your remaining shares, the pending request remains tied to the original address.

## Share price in practice

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does my token balance not increase?">
    Your `ct[Asset]` token count stays the same after deposit. Yield is reflected in the rising share-to-asset exchange rate, not in a growing balance. When you redeem, the vault calculates how many underlying assets each share is worth at that moment and returns that amount to you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I transfer my ct[Asset] tokens?">
    Yes. `ct[Asset]` tokens are standard ERC-20 tokens and can be transferred to any address. Whoever holds the tokens at the time of redemption receives the underlying assets.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What determines how much I receive on withdrawal?">
    The amount you receive equals your `ct[Asset]` share count multiplied by the share-to-asset exchange rate applied to your withdrawal. For vaults with an asynchronous withdrawal queue, this is the exchange rate locked at the time the epoch containing your request is processed. If the vault has earned yield since your deposit, you receive more than you put in; if the vault has experienced losses or fees have accrued, you may receive less.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can the share value go down?">
    Yes. The share-to-asset exchange rate can decrease in two cases: if the vault's strategies report losses, or when management and performance fees accrue (both are paid by minting new shares to the fee recipient, which dilutes existing shareholders). All vault strategies involve risk, including possible loss of principal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
