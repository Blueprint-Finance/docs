When you deposit into a Concrete vault, you receive `ct[Asset]` tokens in return. These tokens are your vault shares — a yield-bearing receipt that proves your proportional ownership of the vault's assets. For example, depositing USDT into the Stable Vault gives you `ctDefiUSDT`, and depositing WBTC gives you `ctWBTC`. The tokens sit in your wallet like any other ERC-20; you do not need to do anything with them for yield to accrue.

## How share price is calculated

Your `ct[Asset]` token balance stays fixed after your deposit. What changes is the value of each token. Share price is calculated as:

```
share price = total vault assets ÷ total shares outstanding
```

As the vault earns yield, total assets increase. With the same number of shares outstanding, each share is worth more. When you eventually withdraw, your `ct[Asset]` tokens are redeemed at the current share price, returning you the original asset plus any yield the vault earned during your deposit period.

## Composability across DeFi

`ct[Asset]` tokens are standard ERC-20 tokens. This means they are composable — usable across DeFi in ways that a locked or illiquid vault position cannot be:

- **Trading** — transfer or trade your vault shares on secondary markets
- **Liquidity provisioning** — use `ct[Asset]` tokens in liquidity pools
- **Leverage** — use vault shares as collateral in lending protocols
- **Structured products** — `ct[Asset]` tokens form the base layer for derivatives and structured products built on Concrete

This composability transforms your vault deposit from a passive, waiting position into an active financial primitive that can be deployed across the ecosystem while still accruing yield from the underlying vault.

## Share price in practice

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does my token balance not increase?">
    Your `ct[Asset]` token count stays the same after deposit. Yield is reflected in the increasing value of each token, not in a growing balance. When you redeem, the vault calculates how many underlying assets each token is worth at that moment and returns that amount to you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I transfer my ct[Asset] tokens?">
    Yes. `ct[Asset]` tokens are standard ERC-20 tokens and can be transferred to any address. Whoever holds the tokens at the time of redemption receives the underlying assets.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What determines how much I receive on withdrawal?">
    The amount you receive equals your `ct[Asset]` token count multiplied by the current share price (total vault assets ÷ total shares outstanding) at the time your withdrawal is processed. If the vault has earned yield since your deposit, you receive more than you put in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if the vault loses value?">
    If vault losses reduce total assets, the share price falls and you would receive less than your initial deposit on withdrawal. All strategies involve risk, including possible loss of principal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
