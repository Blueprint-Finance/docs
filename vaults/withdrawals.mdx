Most Concrete vaults use an asynchronous withdrawal model. Withdrawal requests are grouped into epochs and processed on a schedule, rather than settling instantly. This design protects vault performance: it gives the vault a defined window to deallocate from underlying strategies in an orderly way, avoiding forced unwinds that could compress yield for remaining depositors. Within each epoch, a single share price is locked and applied to all withdrawals in that epoch.

## How the withdrawal queue works

When you submit a withdrawal request, your shares are transferred to the vault and recorded against the current open epoch. They are not burned yet — your position continues to earn yield until the epoch is processed.

At the configured cadence, Concrete's automation closes the current epoch (locking the requests in it), prepares liquidity by deallocating from strategies if needed, and processes the epoch — locking a share price for the epoch, burning the queued shares, and reserving the corresponding assets for claimants. You then claim your assets in a final on-chain step.

The epoch schedule for any specific vault is shown in the Concrete app. Total time from request to claim depends on the vault's cadence and on whether strategy deallocation is needed to source liquidity for the epoch. For example the Concrete DeFi USDT has a seven day withdrawal delay, enabled by two epochs per week.

## Withdrawal steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your vault position">
    Open [app.concrete.xyz/earn](https://app.concrete.xyz/earn) and connect your wallet. Navigate to the vault you want to withdraw from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the withdrawal amount">
    Enter the amount you want to withdraw, or click **Max** to request a full withdrawal. The amount is denominated in the ct\[Asset\]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the withdrawal request">
    Click **Withdraw** and confirm the transaction in your wallet. Your `ct[Asset]` shares are transferred to the vault and queued in the current open epoch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for processing">
    Your request waits until the next epoch close and processing, executed by Concrete's automation. No further action is required from you while this runs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim your assets">
    Once the epoch is processed, you can claim the underlying asset from the vault. After claiming, you no longer hold the corresponding `ct[Asset]` shares.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do my deposits continue earning yield while in the queue?">
    Yes. Your position continues to earn yield until your withdrawal is processed, including across cycles if your request rolls forward due to a withdrawal cap.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel a withdrawal request?">
    Yes, but only before processing has begun. Once your request enters processing at the scheduled cutoff, it can no longer be cancelled.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Processing times depend on the vault's configured cadence and on the time needed to deallocate from underlying strategies. If automation is delayed, requests may be moved to the next epoch by the Withdrawal Manager.
</Note>
