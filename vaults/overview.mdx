Concrete Earn Vaults are automated yield strategies that put your crypto assets to work across DeFi without requiring you to monitor markets, rebalance positions, or interact with individual protocols. You deposit once. From that point, the vault's automation and underlying strategies manage allocation and accounting on your behalf.

## Why Concrete vaults differ from manual DeFi farming

Manual yield farming requires searching across protocols, monitoring APYs, rebalancing positions, paying gas on every action, managing impermanent loss, and reacting to risk around the clock. Concrete consolidates the workflow behind a single deposit.

**Audited, modular architecture.** All vault smart contract code is audited by Halborn (primary retainer) and Cantina (additional audits for core contracts) before deployment, with an ongoing bug bounty program operated by Cantina. The contract system uses EIP-7201 storage and UUPS proxies for safe upgrades.

**Role-based automation.** Concrete vaults separate responsibilities across distinct on-chain roles — Vault Manager, Strategy Manager, Hook Manager, Allocator, and (for async vaults) Withdrawal Manager — each with its own admin role. High-impact governance decisions remain under the vault owner's control while routine operations are handled by Concrete's automation.

**Underlying strategies.** Each vault is configured at deployment with a set of yield-generating strategies — for example, lending markets, liquidity pools, or restaking layers — together with allocation caps and risk parameters. The Allocator role moves capital between the vault and these strategies within the configured rules.

**Independent monitoring and incident response.** Concrete integrates with external systems for accounting verification and security monitoring: 

- **TRES** — off-chain accounting verification for institutional reporting and compliance.
- **Hypernative** — real-time monitoring of vault addresses with alerts on anomalous activity and unexpected transactions. 
- **ZeroShadow** — retained incident response partner with standing authorization to pause vaults when unwind risks are detected.
