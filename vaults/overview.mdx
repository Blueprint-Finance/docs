Concrete Earn Vaults are automated yield strategies that put your crypto assets to work across DeFi without requiring you to monitor markets, rebalance positions, or understand protocol mechanics. You deposit once. Concrete's vault engine then handles allocation across lending markets, liquidity pools, and restaking layers, continuous rebalancing toward the best risk-adjusted opportunities, and automated compounding of rewards. Three vaults are live today, each built on the same audited, institutional-grade infrastructure.

## Why Concrete vaults differ from manual DeFi farming

Manual yield farming requires searching dozens of protocols, monitoring APYs, rebalancing positions, paying gas on every action, managing impermanent loss, and reacting to risk around the clock. Concrete replaces that entire workflow with a single deposit.

**Audited, modular architecture.** All vaults run on a fully modular smart-contract system audited by Halborn and Zellic. The architecture avoids storage collisions and upgrade risks common in legacy contract patterns.

**Active management.** Vaults don't park capital in one place. Capital moves continuously between yield strategies as conditions change — shifting toward better opportunities and pulling back from underperforming ones.

**Role-based automation (Earn V2).** Vault Manager, Allocator, Strategy Manager, and Hook Manager roles separate high-impact governance decisions from routine daily operations, so withdrawals and rebalancing execute without delay while large structural changes remain under tighter oversight.

**Independent oversight.** TRES provides continuous accounting reconciliation. Hypernative delivers real-time monitoring and policy enforcement across all vault operations.
