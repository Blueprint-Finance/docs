Concrete turns a fragmented, multi-step DeFi process into a single action. You choose a vault, deposit your assets, and receive yield-bearing vault share tokens in return. From that point, an institutional-grade infrastructure stack handles allocation, risk management, rebalancing, compounding, and accounting — all without requiring any further action from you.

## The deposit flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Visit [app.concrete.xyz/earn](https://app.concrete.xyz/earn) and connect a compatible Web3 wallet. No account registration is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a vault">
    Browse the available vaults and select one that matches your asset and risk preference — for example, the WBTC Vault for Bitcoin holders, or a stablecoin vault for lower-volatility yield. Each vault displays its supported asset, active strategies, and current exchange rate (eRate).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit your assets">
    Enter the amount you want to deposit and confirm the transaction. Concrete accepts the vault's underlying asset directly — you do not need to bridge, swap, or interact with any other protocol yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive ct[Asset] tokens">
    After your deposit is confirmed, you receive **ct[Asset]** tokens (e.g. ctDefiUSDT, ctWBTC) to your wallet. These are your vault shares. They represent your proportional claim on the vault's total holdings and appreciate in value as the vault earns yield.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn yield automatically">
    Concrete's vault engine deploys your capital across a bundle of yield strategies — lending markets, liquidity pools, restaking layers, and more. The vault continuously rebalances between strategies based on current market conditions and risk parameters. Your ct[Asset] tokens increase in value as the vault's Net Asset Value (NAV) grows. You do not need to claim, compound, or monitor anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a withdrawal">
    When you are ready to exit, submit a withdrawal request. Your request enters a daily processing cycle rather than executing instantly. This orderly queue protects all depositors by allowing the vault to unwind positions carefully rather than selling at unfavorable prices. Typical processing completes within 7 days, though timing may vary based on market conditions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Concrete vaults reward a longer time horizon. Execution costs for entering and exiting yield positions are absorbed by the vault, and compounding requires time to build. Depositors who stay longer benefit from more rebalancing cycles and a greater share of compounded returns.
</Note>

## What happens inside the vault

Once your deposit is in, three layers of the Concrete system work continuously on your behalf.

### Active management

Concrete vaults do not park your capital in a single protocol. The vault engine continuously deploys assets between different yield-generating strategies — shifting toward better opportunities and pulling back from weaker ones as market conditions change. Yield earned in earlier periods is redeployed into new strategies, compounding over time.

### Role-based automation

Concrete's architecture separates responsibilities across distinct roles to prevent any single point of control:

- **Vault Manager** — oversees the overall vault configuration and access controls.
- **Allocator** — executes capital movements between whitelisted protocols within encoded allocation caps.
- **Strategy Manager** (Curator) — manages strategy selection and risk parameters within programmable limits enforced by the vault contract.

Curators manage strategy but not custody. Withdrawals remain under protocol control, not human discretion. Every position is traceable to real, verifiable on-chain holdings.

### Daily NAV updates

Each day, Concrete's systems calculate the vault's Net Asset Value (NAV) — the total dollar value of all positions the vault holds. This figure is recorded on-chain and determines the price of each vault share (eRate). As the NAV rises, your ct[Asset] tokens are worth more.

<Tip>
  The longer you remain deposited, the more rebalancing cycles and compounding periods you capture. A depositor who stays for 12 months benefits from compounding cycles that a 30-day depositor may never see.
</Tip>
