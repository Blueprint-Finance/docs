Concrete's vault strategies draw on a broad set of DeFi protocol integrations, institutional relationships, and security partnerships. DeFi protocols provide the lending markets, restaking infrastructure, and yield mechanisms that vault strategies allocate into. Institutional partners extend Concrete's reach to enterprise participants. Security partners audit the codebase, monitor for threats in real time, and provide independent assessments of protocol risk. The tabs below organize each category.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="DeFi protocols">
    Concrete vault strategies allocate into the lending markets, restaking protocols, yield platforms, and asset issuers listed here. Each integration expands the range of risk-adjusted strategies Concrete can construct and offer to depositors.

    <Note>
      Concrete serves as a curator on both Morpho and Euler. In this role, Concrete designs, deploys, and supervises isolated lending markets structured to institutional standards — not merely allocating into third-party vaults, but defining the collateral parameters, LTV ratios, liquidation mechanics, and risk thresholds of the markets themselves.

      On Morpho, Concrete's initial focus is curating USDC and USDT lending markets against highly liquid collateral — including WBTC and weETH — with conservative loan-to-value ratios and clear liquidation paths. On Euler, Concrete applies the same discipline to isolated credit markets within Euler's modular vault framework.
    </Note>

Concrete vault strategies can allocate directly into these Concrete-curated Morpho vaults, tightening the feedback loop between strategy design and market construction.

Concrete acts as a curator within Euler's vault framework, designing and supervising isolated lending markets structured for institutional participation. Concrete vault strategies may allocate into Concrete-curated Euler markets where appropriate, operating within credit environments whose parameters align with Concrete's internal quantitative framework.
