Concrete vault strategies run across multiple Layer 1 and Layer 2 networks. When you deposit into a vault, Concrete's infrastructure handles routing, bridging, and cross-chain execution behind the scenes — you never need to manually bridge assets, swap gas tokens, or track positions on individual chains. The chains below represent the networks where Concrete vault strategies are active or integrated.

<Note>
  Asset and chain availability depend on current integrations and may change as the ecosystem evolves. Check the [Concrete app](https://app.concrete.xyz/earn) for the latest supported vaults and chains.
</Note>

## How cross-chain execution works

Concrete abstracts all cross-chain complexity at the vault level. When a vault strategy allocates to a protocol on a different chain, Concrete handles:

- Routing capital across chains without requiring manual bridging
- Managing gas on destination chains so you don't need native gas tokens
- Rebalancing and compounding across chains as part of automated strategy execution

You deposit in a single asset on a single chain. Concrete does the rest.
