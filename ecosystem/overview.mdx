Concrete is an Ethereum-based protocol developed by Blueprint Finance — a multi-chain DeFi infrastructure company that also builds Glow Finance on Solana. Concrete's vault infrastructure draws on a broad ecosystem of DeFi protocols, Layer 1 and Layer 2 networks, institutional partners, and security firms. These integrations let Concrete vaults allocate capital across strategies, manage risk systematically, and operate at institutional standards. You never need to manually bridge assets, switch networks, or manage cross-chain complexity.

## Ecosystem categories

Concrete's ecosystem spans four main categories. Each plays a distinct role in how vaults are built, secured, and operated.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="DeFi / CeFi protocols" icon="chart-line">
    Lending protocols (Morpho, Euler, Aave, Compound, Radiant, Silo), yield platforms (Pendle), restaking protocols (EigenLayer, Renzo, Symbiotic), liquid staking (Bedrock, Kelp), and asset issuers (Ethena, WBTC, Agora, Dinero) that Concrete vault strategies allocate into.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Institutional partners" icon="building-2">
    Wallet providers, custodians, and asset managers — including Binance Wallet, Bitget Wallet, BiT Global, Maple, Lorenzo, and Tres — that extend Concrete's reach to institutional participants.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Security partners" icon="shield-check">
    Audit firms and real-time monitoring services — including Halborn, Zellic, Trail of Bits, Cantina, Code4rena, Hypernative, ZeroShadow, and Test Machine — that continuously protect the Concrete stack.
  </Card>
  <Card title="L1 & L2 chains" icon="layers">
    The blockchains where Concrete vaults deploy strategies: Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Berachain, BNB Chain, Corn, Morph, Movement, Avalanche, and more.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Concrete is part of the Blueprint Finance ecosystem. Blueprint Finance is the core developer of both Concrete (Ethereum-based) and Glow Finance (Solana-based). Concrete's ecosystem documentation covers the Ethereum side only.
