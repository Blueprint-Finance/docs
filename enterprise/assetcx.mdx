AssetCX lets institutional asset holders earn yield through Concrete's vault infrastructure while assets remain in the custody of their existing centralized finance platform. If your organization cannot move funds directly into DeFi — due to regulatory requirements, internal policy, or counterparty constraints — AssetCX provides a path to yield generation that works within those boundaries.

## How it works

Rather than requiring you to move assets on-chain yourself, AssetCX integrates with your custodian so that assets stay under their safekeeping while Concrete's infrastructure generates yield. The assets you hold remain in custody; the yield opportunity does not.

## Supported assets

AssetCX supports yield generation on the following asset types:

- **BTC**
- **ETH**
- **SOL**
- **Stablecoins** — USDT, USDC, USD1

<Note>
  AssetCX requires an enquiry and qualification process. Not all asset and custodian combinations are immediately available. The Concrete team reviews each application individually.
</Note>

## How to apply

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the AssetCX enquiry page">
    Navigate to [concrete.xyz/#assetcx](https://concrete.xyz/#assetcx) to find the AssetCX enquiry form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the enquiry form">
    Provide your email address, select the asset type you hold (BTC, ETH, SOL, or stablecoins), choose your custodian from the list, and include any additional details about your situation. You will also confirm whether you are enquiring on behalf of a qualified custodian.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and wait for a response">
    After submitting, the Concrete team will review your enquiry and contact you to discuss eligibility, available yield options, and next steps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also reach the AssetCX enquiry form directly from the Concrete app. Visit [app.concrete.xyz/earn](https://app.concrete.xyz/earn) and look for the AssetCX section to start your application.
</Tip>
