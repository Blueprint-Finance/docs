From initial vault configuration through daily automated operations, Concrete handles the full lifecycle — so you can focus on strategy while Concrete manages execution, accounting, and monitoring.

## What sets Concrete apart

Most vault platforms leave critical operational gaps. Concrete Enterprise closes them.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Daily NAV updates">
    Concrete is the only vault platform with automated daily NAV updates. Exchange rates, epoch closings, and withdrawal processing all execute on a fixed daily schedule through a three-party automation cycle — no manual intervention required. See the [Vault Setup](/enterprise/vault-setup) page for details on how this works.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="On-chain and off-chain accounting">
    Every vault comes with integrated accounting through TRES, providing verifiable reporting that meets institutional standards. On-chain positions are reconciled continuously and available for independent inspection at any time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Integrates with your wallet setup">
    Concrete Enterprise works with your existing custody and signing infrastructure. Bring your own wallets, multisigs, and data sources — the platform adapts to your operational setup rather than forcing you to change it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The two-phase deployment model

Concrete Enterprise follows a structured deployment process that takes your vault from configuration to live operations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Phase 1 — Vault setup">
    Configure roles and permissions for your team, choose between a hosted UI or SDK integration, and connect your strategy, wallets, and data sources. Concrete's flexible role structure accommodates the governance requirements of protocols, funds, and DAOs of any size.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Phase 2 — Running the vault">
    Once live, deposits, withdrawals, and rebalancing run on autopilot. Exchange rates and yield accounting update daily. Monitor positions and performance in real time, and add new strategies at any time without downtime.
  </Step>
</Steps>
