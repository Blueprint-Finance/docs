The Berachain Bitcoin LBTC and WBTC vaults have been migrated and are now fully managed by Lombard. 

Check [Lombard Discord](https://discord.com/invite/2HG7G69twc) for the latest updates and support.  
   
The Vault’s strategies include liquidity provisioning on DEXs, money markets, and Pendle. Sentora deploys a variety of advanced DeFi strategies, generating yield on non-BTC opportunities by borrowing stablecoins or ETH with LBTC collateral.

All strategies are managed by Sentora’s institutional-grade risk management platform.

The Vault facilitates automated compounding and efficiently returns yield in LBTC or WBTC. Any additional rewards will be claimable separately.
