The **Berachain Stables Vaults** provide access to diversified, low-volatility strategies for stablecoin holders. Funds are allocated across partner protocols including **Pendle**, **Euler**, **Dolomite**, **Kodiak, Bex, Beraborrow** for optimized APY.

- Multi-asset support including USDe, sUSDe, HONEY, USDC.e 
- Blend of time-locked and liquid yield strategies 
- Full alignment with Concrete’s withdrawal queue and Points system 
- Integrated with Bera-native partners for ecosystem synergy

For more on how Concrete manages risk—including impermanent loss, slippage, and smart contract security, [read our Risk and Safety article](https://docs.concrete.xyz/risks).

## Dynamic APY calculation



This vault's headline APY is computed dynamically from on-chain accrual
and a recent strategy-performance window. The figure refreshes between
visits and reflects the most recently observed performance rather than a
static target value.

See [Dynamic APY calculation](/concepts/earn/vaults/dynamic-apy-concept#dynamic-apy-calculation) for the inputs and
the way the calculation is derived.





A historical APY chart renders on this vault's page so you can see how
the yield has evolved over time. The chart is independent of the
headline-APY source.
