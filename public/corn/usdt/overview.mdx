**[View Corn Stables on the Earn app →](https://app.concrete.xyz/vault/corn/usdt)**

The Corn Stables Vault is a high-potential DeFi opportunity on Ethereum, created in partnership with Corn and Tether.

Deposit USDT directly to start earning yield. USDC holders can seamlessly swap to USDT using the built-in interface.

Deposited funds are bridged to the Corn network and deployed across integrated strategies like Curve and lending protocols to optimize yield.

Rewards will be distributed via [Merkl.xyz](https://app.merkl.xyz/opportunities/ethereum/ERC20/0x3Eb6464A77D7B619AaAfa7e9FFC0fBe3ed7084B3).

Withdrawals from this vault are processed in batches. Once a batch is complete, funds are sent directly to users' addresses on Corn.
