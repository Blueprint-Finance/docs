## Deposit disable


New deposits into the vault are disabled in the UI. The deposit form is
either hidden or rendered in a disabled state communicating that the vault
is no longer accepting capital.

Existing positions are unaffected: holders can still withdraw, claim, and
read state. Disabling deposits is the standard mechanism for soft-closing a
vault before deprecation, freezing a vault prior to a migration, or
responding to a temporary external constraint.




Deposit disable is independent of withdrawal disable. A vault commonly has
deposits disabled while withdrawals remain enabled — that combination
describes a vault winding down toward deprecation.
