title: "Corn Bitcoin — Overview"
sidebarTitle: "Overview"
---

Concrete Corn Bitcoin Vaults allow deposits of uniBTC, LBTC, and solvBTC. Deposited assets are then bridged to Corn and deposited across exchanges and lending markets (Curve, Avalon, ZeroLend) to maximise yield and rewards.

Withdrawals are available on Ethereum mainnet.
