The **Berabaddies Earn Vault** is designed to support the Berachain ecosystem with minimal complexity and high accessibility. Deposits into this vault are put to work through straightforward mechanisms that maximize onchain participation and BGT utility.

Vault yield comes primarily from yield-boosting strategies such as leveraging integrated partner platforms like Infrared and converting earned BGT to BERA/HONEY to enhance APR. Rewards are auto-compounded back into the vault—realized as WBERA or HONEY yield—allowing depositors to benefit directly without needing to claim a separate reward token.  
  
**Vault APY: **  
- WBERA: 126.88%  
- HONEY: 5.13%

**Key Features:**

- **Flexible reward structure** → Yield-boosting mechanisms include leveraging Infrared and converting earned BGT to BERA/HONEY.
- **Concrete ecosystem native** → Fully integrated with Concrete Points and vault architecture.
- **Withdrawal queue applies** → Withdrawals processed after 7-day queue.

For more on how Concrete manages risk—including impermanent loss, slippage, and smart contract security, [read our Risk and Safety article](https://docs.concrete.xyz/risks).
