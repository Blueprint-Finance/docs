The **Arbitrum Drip Vaults** provide access to expertly curated looping strategies designed to maximize participation in Arbitrum's Drip campaign. Each vault employs asset looping across participating venues to amplify rewards and optimize capital efficiency.  
  
**Available Vaults:**

- **USDe Vault** - Curated USDe looping strategies
- **SyrupUSDC Vault** - Optimized SyrupUSDC positioning
- **ezETH Vault** - Restaked ETH yield amplification

**How funds are handled:** Deposits are allocated through **curated strategies** across participating Arbitrum protocols. Each vault uses targeted looping mechanisms specific to its underlying asset, maximizing exposure to **Drip campaign rewards**. All positions utilize Concrete's **audited Earn V1** infrastructure with **non-custodial** asset management.
