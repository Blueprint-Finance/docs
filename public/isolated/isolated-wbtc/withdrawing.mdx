## Epoch lifecycle


Withdrawals requested by Fridays 12:01 PM are processed and made available for claiming the following Friday at 12:00 PM UTC.

Requests submitted after the cutoff roll into the next epoch instead.
Closed-pending requests settle on-chain and become claimable when the
next epoch starts.

See [Withdrawal epoch lifecycle](/concepts/earn/withdrawals/epoch-cycle-concept) for the full
explanation of how epochs start, close, and settle.

## Queue position numbering



This vault has no cap configured, so every request queues into its
native epoch and settles in FIFO order within that epoch. See
[Queue position](/concepts/earn/withdrawals/queue-position-concept) for the full mechanics.
