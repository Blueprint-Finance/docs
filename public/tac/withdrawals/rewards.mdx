## Rewards display

Vaults can advertise additional **rewards** beyond their base yield —
typically point programs, third-party token incentives, or campaign
multipliers. Rewards are CMS-authored and rendered as badges on the vault
card and vault page.

Each reward has:

- **Name** — short label shown to users (e.g. "Eigen Points").
- **Token icon** — the token or program the reward is denominated in.
  Rendered as an icon next to the name.
- **Value** — optional numeric multiplier or rate. When numeric, the UI
  parses and displays it; otherwise the raw string is shown.

Rewards displayed in the UI are descriptive. They do not imply automatic
distribution by the vault contract — the rules for accrual and claiming
are determined by the program operator and communicated separately.
