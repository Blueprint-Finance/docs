**[View Morph BGB on the Earn app →](https://app.concrete.xyz/vault/morph/bgb)**

Deposit BGB on Morph to get access to optimal DeFi yields in the Morph ecosystem. Active rewards will vary over time as the vault allocates to different protocols. There will be an initial locked deposit period before the vault starts deploying funds into Morph protocols on a rolling basis. Vault can allocate to [Bulbaswap](https://bulbaswap.io/), [Quokka Lend](https://quokka.finance/) and [Layerbank](https://layerbank.finance/) with more under consideration.
