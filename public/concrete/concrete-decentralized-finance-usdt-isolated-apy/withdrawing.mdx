## Epoch lifecycle

submitted after the cutoff roll into the next epoch instead. Closed-pending
requests settle on-chain and become claimable when the next epoch starts.

## Queue position numbering



This vault has no cap configured, so every request queues into its
native epoch and settles in FIFO order within that epoch. See
[Queue position](/concepts/earn/withdrawals/queue-position-concept#queue-position) for the full mechanics.
