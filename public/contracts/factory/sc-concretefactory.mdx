## ConcreteFactory

Deploys and tracks every Concrete vault. Approves and blocks vault
        implementations, records per-vault migration paths, predicts CREATE2 proxy
        addresses, and stores the protocol-wide enforced default vault configuration.

_Integrators query this factory to enumerate vaults and resolve implementation
     versions. It is itself a UUPS-upgradeable contract owned by the protocol._
