## SimpleStrategy

A simple strategy implementation that holds assets without generating yield.
        Serves as a concrete implementation for strategies that simply hold assets
        and can be used for testing or as a fallback strategy.

_Uses EIP-7201 storage layout for upgradeability and caches deposit balance in storage._
