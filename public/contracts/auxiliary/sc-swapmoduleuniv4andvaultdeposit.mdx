## SwapModuleUniV4AndVaultDeposit

Swap module for bidirectional swaps between an ERC-4626 vault share token and its
        underlying asset. The underlying-to-shares direction is fulfilled by depositing or
        minting through the vault itself; the shares-to-underlying direction is fulfilled
        by swapping on a Uniswap V4 pool via UniversalRouter + Permit2.
Designed for looping strategies that repeatedly deposit underlying assets to receive
        shares and then swap shares back to underlying to compound returns. The module pulls
        tokens from `msg.sender`, executes the trade, and forwards the proceeds, holding zero
        balance after each call as an invariant.

_Architecture summary:
     - Underlying to shares uses `IERC4626.deposit` (exact-in) or `IERC4626.mint` (exact-out).
     - Shares to underlying uses the Uniswap V4 `UniversalRouter`; allowance to the router
       is delegated through Permit2, with max amount and max expiration set at construction.
     - The shares-to-underlying paths read the borrow balance before and after the swap to
       exclude any pre-existing donations._
