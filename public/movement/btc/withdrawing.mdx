## Withdrawal disable


Withdrawals from the vault are disabled in the UI. The withdraw form is
either hidden or rendered in a disabled state.

The most common reason to disable withdrawals is a pre-deposit vault before
the L2 claim window opens (see [pre-deposit claim](cross-chain#pre-deposit-share-claiming)).
Other cases include migrations and contract-level limit changes that make
withdraws non-functional.
