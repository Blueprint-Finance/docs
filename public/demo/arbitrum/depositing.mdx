## Deposit disable




The vault accepts deposits according to its on-chain limits and any
permissioning rules.


Deposit disable is independent of withdrawal disable. A vault commonly has
deposits disabled while withdrawals remain enabled — that combination
describes a vault winding down toward deprecation.
