To achieve the maximum yield, the Morpho USD vault automates USDC deposits across multiple Morpho vaults, operated by [Gauntlet](https://www.gauntlet.xyz/), [MEV Capital](https://mevcapital.com/), [RE7 Labs](https://re7.capital/) and [Steakhouse Financial](https://www.steakhouse.financial/projects/risk-management-framework-for-defi-protocols). We have decided not to allocate any liquidity to MEV Capital Usual USDC due to unclear communication from the Usual Team. For more on Concrete, [read our docs](https://docs.concrete.xyz/Overview/welcome).

Vault rewards are included in the APY. Points are earned additionally.  
A withdrawal queue of 72 hours will be implemented soon for this vault.

## Dynamic APY calculation





### Institutional APY

This vault uses an institutional APY. The strategy or the allocated
assets are operated by Partners, so the headline cannot be assessed
dynamically the same way as a Concrete-run strategy. Instead the headline
shows the pinned target rate (`aprTarget` in the CMS).

The displayed APY is stable between CMS updates and changes only when
Concrete updates the target value. It is intended to represent the rate
the Partner has communicated for the strategy, not a live measurement of
recent on-chain performance.
