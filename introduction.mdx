Concrete is a suite of DeFi products built to generate automated yield on any on-chain asset. Instead of navigating dozens of protocols, monitoring APYs, rebalancing manually, and paying gas at every turn, you make a single deposit and Concrete's vault infrastructure handles everything else — allocation, rebalancing, compounding, and risk management — continuously, on your behalf.

Concrete is designed for two audiences: **liquidity providers** who want to maximize yield without becoming DeFi experts, and **institutional allocators** who require the segregation of duties, auditable transparency, and governed architecture that professional capital demands.

## What you get

- **One deposit, multiple strategies.** Each vault bundles lending, liquidity provisioning, restaking, and other yield sources into a single product.
- **Automated optimization.** Concrete's quantitative models continuously rotate capital toward the best risk-adjusted opportunities and away from underperforming ones.
- **ct[Asset] vault shares.** When you deposit, you receive yield-bearing tokens (e.g. ctDefiUSDT, ctWBTC) that represent your share of the vault and appreciate as yield accrues.
- **No manual rebalancing.** You never need to bridge, swap, claim rewards, or interact with individual protocols.

<Note>
  Yields are not guaranteed and may fluctuate. You may lose some or all of your deposited assets. Past performance is not indicative of future results.
</Note>
